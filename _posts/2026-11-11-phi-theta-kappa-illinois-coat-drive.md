The KCC Alpha Delta Eta Chapter of Phi Theta Kappa is proud to participate in the Phi Theta Kappa Illinois Coat Drive!

As temperatures drop, we have an opportunity to make a difference for individuals and families in need. Your generosity can help provide warmth to someone this season.

**Want to make a difference?** You can participate by making a monetary donation through the [PTK One Warm Coat donation page](https://onewarmcoat.rallybound.org/communitydrives/Member/MyPage/10683406/Lauren-Benson){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Every $1 donated can help warm one person!
