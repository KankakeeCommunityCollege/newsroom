KCC students are invited to join KCC’s 3rd annual Wiffle Ball® Tournament on Friday, Sept 25 at 1 p.m.

Students can join faculty/staff in this fun event. Here’s how it works:

* [Sign up to play or volunteer here](https://forms.gle/WU2HVymwd4WF6yJ37 "Sign up to play or volunteer for Whiffle Ball"){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}!
  * Sign up as an individual.
  * Teams will be selected randomly.
  * Volunteer opportunities include umpires, scorekeepers, and snack duty.
* The final day to register is Thursday, Sept. 17.
* Everyone who signs up will receive information about teams, game times, and rules.

Questions regarding the event should be directed to Tierra Dixon-Sullivan, [tdixon-sullivan@kcc.edu](mailto:tdixon-sullivan@kcc.edu "email to tdixon-sullivan@kcc.edu"){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

There will be hot dogs and other snacks/refreshments for players and spectators. Come out, cheer for your favorite team, and celebrate the first week of fall!
