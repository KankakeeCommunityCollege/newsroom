The public is invited to a gallery talk for the exhibit "Life Through The Lens ... A Photographic Retrospective by Bill 'BJ' Jurevich" at noon Wednesday, Sept. 2 in the lower-level gallery area of KCC's Miner Memorial Library.

![Photo of Annie from 1995](/uploads/2023/annie250x264.png "Annie 1995"){: width="250" height="264"}

The exhibit features 24 photos from Jurevich's photography career.

"I want guests to realize what a privilege it has been for me entering the lives of so many people in this community over the past 40 years," Jurevich said. "I have attempted to honestly portray moments, albeit, private slices of life which informed, entertained and possibly inspired the viewer.

"The documentary images, uncontrolled moments in time, reflect my respect for the subjects. My commercial photography strives to be as technically perfect as possible while offering personal creativity pleasing to the subject."

Jurevich is owner and photographer for Image Group Photography, LLC, which he founded in 1986. He is also KCC's contract photographer (since 1986); an adjunct professor at Olivet Nazarene University in Bourbonnais; and president of the board for the local non-profit Wright in Kankakee.

The exhibit is open during library hours until Sept. 25:

* 7:30 a.m.-7 p.m. Mondays and Tuesdays;
* 7:30 a.m.-7 p.m. Wednesdays and Thursdays; and
* 7:30 a.m.-7 p.m. Fridays.

KCC is located at 100 College Drive in Kankakee.
