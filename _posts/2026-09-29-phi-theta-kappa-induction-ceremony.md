Twenty-nine Kankakee Community College students were inducted into Phi Theta Kappa Honor Society in September 2026.

KCC students who recently joined are:

* **Bourbonnais**: Savannah Fabsits, Livia Lamie, Joseph Roiger, Aysia Whitman, Mia Whitman and Lysette Williams.
* **Bradley**: Aubrianna Updegraff.
* **Brimfield**: Ava Simpson.
* **Chicago**: Jasmine Thurmond.
* **Clifton**: Lexi Willms.
* **Essex**: Krystina Morman.
* **Grant Park**: Seth Dobin.
* **Gurnee**: Alan Arguelles.
* **Kankakee**: Ryan Graf, Brooke King, Jillian Laird, Stephen Mercer, Kathryn Mullins, Ella Nitschke-Wern and Jessica Stano.
* **Manteno**: Madison Anderson, Kaylee Boudreau, Keeley Mazurkiewicz and Angelina Urban.
* **Momence**: Justin Shafer and Cassidy Thompson.
* **Park Forest**: Candice Jolivette.
* **Watseka**: Dorothy Harms and Noelle Schroeder.

KCC holds induction ceremonies for new [Phi Theta Kappa](https://www.kcc.edu/student-resources/clubs/#phi-theta-kappa) members each fall and spring semester. This fall’s event was Oct. 24.

To be eligible for membership, students must have a 3.5 grade point average or better on a 4.0 scale after completing a minimum of 12 semesters hours of KCC coursework.

For more information on the [Alpha Delta Eta chapter at KCC](https://www.kcc.edu/student-resources/student-life/clubs/#phi-theta-kappa), contact a chapter adviser, Jaclyn Montemayer or Kenneth West, at [jmontemayer@kcc.edu](mailto:jmontemayer@kcc.edu) or [kwest@kcc.edu](mailto:kwest@kcc.edu).

**Sidebar: About Phi Theta Kappa**

Phi Theta Kappa is the premier honor society recognizing the academic achievement of students at associate degree-granting colleges and helping them to grow as scholars and leaders. The honor society has more than 3.8 million members and nearly 1,300 chapters in 11 countries, with approximately 220,000 active members in the nation’s colleges. Learn more at [www.kcc.edu/student-resources/clubs/#phi-theta-kappa](http://www.kcc.edu/student-resources/clubs/#phi-theta-kappa).
