The Miner Memorial Library staff has decorated the Quiet Study Room as a Spider-Infested Study Room 🕷️.

It's open October 29 & October 30 so that students and staff can enjoy it for two days before the weekend. Come visit today or tomorrow, and maybe bring some pals!

![Spooky eyes and spider web](/uploads/2023/img-20261029-082221184-248x186-1.jpg "Spooky eyes and spider web"){: width="248" height="186"}

Don't let the caution signs stop you... if you make it through the room, you might find a treat 🙂. It's on the east end of the Library, lower level.
