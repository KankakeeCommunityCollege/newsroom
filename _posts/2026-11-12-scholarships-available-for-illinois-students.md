There are a number of scholarships and grants sponsored by Illinois government agencies. Please use the links below to find them.

* [John Lewis Youth Leadership Award](https://www.ilsos.gov/special/john-lewis-award.html "John Lewis You"){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
* [Illinois Student Assistance Commission Scholarships](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/index.html "Illinois Scholarship Programs"){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}<br />Programs denoted with an asterisk (\*) are available to undergraduate students only, and programs with a double-asterisk (\*\*) are available to graduate students only. All other programs are for both undergraduate and graduate students.
  * [Illinois Special Education Teacher Tuition Waiver (SETTW) Program](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/illinois-special-education-teacher-tuition-waiver-settw-program.html)
  * [Early Childhood Access Consortium for Equity (ECACE) Scholarship Program](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/ecace.html "Early Childhood Access Consortium for Equity &#40;ECACE&#41; Scholarship Program") \*
  * [Displaced Energy Worker Dependent Transition Scholarship Program](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/DEWDTSP.html "Displaced Energy Worker Dependent Transition Scholarship Program")
  * [School Social Work Scholarship](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/P-MSSWSP.html) \*\*
  * [Golden Apple Scholars of Illinois](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/golden-apple-scholars-of-illinois.html) \*
  * [Deceased, Disabled, and MIA-POW Veterans’ Dependents Scholarship](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/mia-pow-scholarship.html)
  * [Nursing Education Scholarship](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/nursing-education-scholarship-program.html)
  * [Illinois Graduate and Retain our Workforce (iGROW) Tech Scholarship Program](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/iGROW.html) \*
  * [Teachers of Illinois Scholarship Program](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/TISP.html)
  * [GEAR UP Scholarship](https://www.isac.org/students/during-college/types-of-financial-aid/scholarships/gear-up-scholarship.html) \*

Consult the individual scholarship information for application instructions and deadlines. Other scholarships may be available.

This message is being posted as a community service. The scholarships are not offered by KCC.

**To learn about scholarships offered by KCC**, visit [www.kcc.edu/scholarships](www.kcc.edu/scholarships "www.kcc.edu/scholarships"){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. The KCC scholarship application is open every year from Oct. 1 to May 1.
