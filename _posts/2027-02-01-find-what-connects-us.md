This is a message from KCC’s Culture of Caring Committee.

![Two st](/uploads/2023/make-connections-20260928-z91-9893.jpg)

Let’s create situations where there is common ground and inclusion. Belonging grows when we recognize the experiences we share. Inclusion demonstrates that every person matters and has a place in our community.

Here are some activities that can foster connections and inclusion:

* Find three things you have in common with someone you don’t know well.
* Intentionally include others in conversations.
* Invite someone new to join a group or activity.
* Introduce people to one another.

By doing this, you aren’t just transforming someone else’s day—you can deepen your own sense of belonging. When we choose to look up, reach out, and truly see one another, we build a college culture where no one is a stranger.

Thank you for bringing your kindness, your presence, and your voice to KCC! Let’s make this our shared commitment: to ensure that every single person who walks our halls knows they belong here.
